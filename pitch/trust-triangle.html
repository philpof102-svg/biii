The trust triangle

Three signals no single party can fake, composed into one verdict.

Each corner already exists in the stack. BIII’s job is the composition — and it fails closed: the absence of a signal is never read as trust.

UNSAFE
A flag on the counterparty overrides every other green. Never pay.
UNKNOWN
No positive signal. Proceed with caution — absence is not trust.
TRUSTED
Vetted to pay: reputation safe or proven standing, before settlement.
SETTLED
The payment is proven real on-chain. The strongest, top state.

One call

An agent — or your app — asks once, and gets the whole triangle back.

The verdict is composable and deterministic. Any partner can wire their own oracle into a corner; the fail-closed logic is the same code, offline-testable (24/24).
